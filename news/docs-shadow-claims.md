* `boost_shadow()` and the article *Four Figures From One boostmtree Fit* no
  longer state a false-positive rate for the shadow band. Shadows in a copy
  share one subject shuffle and keep the covariates' correlations, so the
  band is a reference measured in the fit, not a test with a known error
  rate. The article's seed advice now says the fixed-seed subsample defect
  was fixed in `boostmtree` 2.0.13.
